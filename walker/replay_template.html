What you're looking at

the cast
FLY — the real brain MaleCNS v1.0, the adult male fruit-fly connectome: 166,700 neurons, 25.6M connections. At every junction it views each exit's street photo through a simulated retina and turns the connectome's spike traffic into a direction score. This is the experiment: does the connectome walk?
COIN — no brain Picks a uniformly random exit at every step. The noise floor: a walker that does not beat COIN is not navigating.
GREEDY — no brain Always steps toward the exit that most reduces the straight-line distance to the goal. The sense-of-direction ceiling: how far FLY is from intent.
the legend, decoded
the 3D stage
FLY where it actually stood at this tick, on its capture point, turned to the heading it walked. Around it: the capture lattice (street photos as dots, neighbours linked), the three walker trails, and a gold beam over the nata.
junction theater (map)
only the street graph near where the walkers actually went. Dots are street-photo capture points; thin lines link neighbouring captures.
GREEDY ghost path
the dashed blue line: shortest graph route from GREEDY's last position to the goal.
approach scores A–E, in Hz
the exits from the current junction. Bar length is the connectome's score for that exit's photo, in Hz = spikes per second in the descending neurons the decoder listens to. Higher, stronger approach vote; ▼ marks the exit FLY actually took.
BUY / SELL / HOLD
the DNp20 decoder's verdict: right-selective neurons firing harder (BUY — move right-leaning), left harder (SELL — left-leaning), neither (HOLD).
Σ spikes · gate · latency
total connectome spikes across the consults, DNpe017 gate spike count, and how long the brain simulation took for that candidate.
delta pill & reinforcement
±m closer / away: this step's change in straight-line distance to the nata. The dopamine badge means progress queued a reward pulse for the next consult; aversive means regression.
